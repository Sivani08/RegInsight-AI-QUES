# Two-minute Life Sciences explanation

“RegInsight helps regulatory and quality teams move from a portfolio signal to the source evidence behind it.

The starting point is inspection history and observation text. The application organizes those records into a filtered view of inspection activity, outcomes, recurring themes and site-prioritization indicators. It keeps different types of data separate: an inspection count, an observation row and an annual industry citation frequency do not mean the same thing.

Most of the intelligence is transparent calculation. The software computes counts, rates and risk factors from the available records and shows when information is missing. AI contributes a source-linked explanation and, when configured, observation-text classification. It does not turn an analytical score into a regulatory judgment.

A user can ask why a site stands out, inspect the records supporting the answer, compare related observations and document a review decision. The local chatbot retrieves evidence first. If its model is unavailable or its output fails checks, the application shows the evidence instead of pretending to have a verified AI answer.

The intended benefit is less time spent finding and reconciling evidence, and a clearer basis for deciding what to investigate next. It supports preparation and prioritization. It does not certify compliance, replace expert judgment or execute a complete CAPA process.

The same architecture also retains optional clinical QC monitoring APIs and searchable supplied references. Those are separate supporting capabilities, not claims that this is a complete clinical or quality management platform.”
